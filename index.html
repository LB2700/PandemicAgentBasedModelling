Agent Based Pandemic Modelling

Credit to 3blue1brown's video Simulating an Epidemic. This project is meant to be used as a playground to tinker and see how different behviour's or types of viruses affect the spread through a population. The data and results from this tool are up to the discretion of the user to deterrmine they're relevancy or accuracy. As in 3blue1brown's video, this model is based on the SIR model. Suseptible. Infected. Recovered. Infected agents transmit the disease to the suseptible agents. Recovered agents cannot contract the virus a second time. Green represents suseptible, red represents infected, and yellow is recovered.

Above on the right is a chart depicting the portions of the population in the SIR model. Generally the red portion resembles a bell curve where there is a sudden surge in contractions and then it tapers off. For a goverment to look after its population it must try to minimize the final yellow region

The population density is determined not by the size of the population, nor by the on-screen size of the boundry. The scale of the simulation is simply shifted up or down to represent different population densities. Think of it as zooming in on the camera on your phone, everything on your screen gets bigger but the screen itself stays the same size!

Virus and Location presets are not based off of real life data!
